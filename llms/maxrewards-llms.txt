# MaxRewards

> MaxRewards helps you manage your credit cards and maximize rewards. Track spending, automatically activate card offers, compare credit cards, and get personalized recommendations to earn more cash back and points.

## Credit cards

- [Credit cards](https://maxrewards.com/credit-cards): Browse credit cards with detailed rewards rates, welcome offers, benefits, and fee breakdowns.
- [Best credit cards](https://maxrewards.com/credit-cards/best): Curated best-card lists by category — cash back, travel, no annual fee, business, and by issuer.
